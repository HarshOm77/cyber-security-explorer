Types of Cyber Threats

Learn about common online dangers

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails or messages designed to trick people into sharing passwords or personal information.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software designed to harm devices, disrupt systems or steal information.

🔒

Ransomware

A type of malware that locks or encrypts files and demands payment.

👤

Identity Theft

When someone uses another person's personal information without permission.

💥

DoS Attacks

Attempts to make a website or online service unavailable to its users.

🔑

Password Attacks

Attempts to gain unauthorized access to accounts by compromising passwords.

Stay Safe Online

Follow these important cybersecurity habits

🔑

Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords for different accounts.

📱

Enable 2FA

Use two-factor authentication wherever available.

🚫

Avoid Suspicious Links

Don't open unexpected links or attachments from unknown senders.

🔄

Update Devices

Keep your operating system and apps updated.
